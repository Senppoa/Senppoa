# Kun Tang

**Computational chemistry · Reactive MLIPs · AI for Science**

Chemical Engineering PhD candidate at Dalian University of Technology · Expected 2027

I work at the intersection of quantum chemistry, reactive machine-learning potentials, and computer-aided molecular design. My focus is building reproducible workflows for transition-state search and organometallic catalyst design, connecting computational models with experimentally testable chemistry.
